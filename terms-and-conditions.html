Rental Terms & Conditions

Yes365 Rental Management Services Pty Ltd (ACN 673 976 719), 2 Holbeach Avenue, Tempe NSW 2044.

Last updated: 26/08/2026

1. Eligibility

Yes365 rents vehicles for use on rideshare platforms (Uber, DiDi), app-based food delivery (e.g. UberEats) and personal use. Applicants must meet all applicable requirements:

Australian licence holders

  • Australian (NSW) driver licence, full or P2. P1 licences are not accepted, regardless of age;
  • Drivers under 25 (holding a full or P2 licence) may be accepted subject to individual assessment at the in-person interview.

International licence holders

  • Valid international driver licence;
  • Current passport;
  • An Australian bank account in the applicant's name.

An ABN or an approved rideshare platform account is not required at the time of rental.

Final approval requires a mandatory in-person interview at the Yes365 office in Tempe, where original documents must be presented. Yes365 reserves the right to decline any application.

2. Rental period and payment

  • Rental is weekly, paid in advance. The rental week starts on the day the vehicle is collected, and each following week falls due on the same day of the week (e.g. collect on a Wednesday, renewal falls due every Wednesday).
  • All amounts are in Australian dollars (AUD) and include 10% GST.
  • On collection of the vehicle, the driver pays the bond + the first week in advance.
  • Payment is made by direct debit or PayTo via Monova. An active payment mandate is a mandatory condition for collecting the vehicle.
  • If a debit fails, further attempts will be made during the week. Persistent non-payment may result in suspension of the agreement and recovery of the vehicle.
  • Late payment: after a 24-hour grace period, a daily fee of 1/10 of the weekly rate applies; part days are charged at 1/10 of the daily fee per hour.

3. Bond

  • AUD 500, drivers holding a full Australian licence, aged 25 or over;
  • AUD 1,000, all other cases (under 25, P2 licence or international licence).
  • The bond is refunded within 2 weeks of the vehicle being returned, less any amounts owing (damage, fines, tolls, fees or outstanding balance).

4. Insurance and excess

  • Vehicles are covered by rideshare-use insurance through P2P Cover Limited (p2pcover.com.au).
  • In the event of a claim, the following excess applies:
    • AUD 1,500, drivers holding a full Australian licence, aged 25 or over;
    • AUD 2,500, drivers under 25, P2 licence or international licence.
  • The excess may be charged to the driver where the claim arises from use of the vehicle during the rental, as set out in the rental agreement.

5. Use of the vehicle

  • The vehicle may be used for passenger transport on rideshare platforms (Uber, DiDi), app-based food delivery (e.g. UberEats) and the contracting driver's personal use.
  • Use of the vehicle for freight or cargo transport is prohibited.
  • Only the contracting driver may drive the vehicle. Sub-letting or lending the vehicle to third parties is prohibited.
  • The vehicle must not be used for unlawful purposes, racing, towing or the transport of dangerous goods.
  • Smoking in the vehicle is prohibited (cleaning fee applies, see Section 7).
  • The driver must hold a valid driver licence throughout the rental and immediately notify Yes365 of any suspension or cancellation of their licence. Loss or suspension of a rideshare platform account does not need to be notified, the driver may continue using the vehicle for personal use.
  • Vehicles are fitted with a GPS tracking device for fleet security and recovery, as described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Driver obligations

  • Keep the vehicle clean and in good condition;
  • Present the vehicle for scheduled maintenance when requested by Yes365;
  • Immediately report any accident, damage, mechanical fault, fine or seizure;
  • Not carry out repairs without Yes365's authorisation;
  • Refuel with the correct fuel type.

7. Tolls, fines and administration fees

Amounts charged to the driver's account, GST inclusive:

ItemAmount
Traffic fine administration feeAUD 25 per infringement
Toll administration feeAUD 10 + the amount of each toll
Cleaning (vehicle returned dirty)AUD 150, or AUD 250 if there is a cigarette odour
Key replacementAUD 500
Missing fuel on returnAUD 4 per litre
Tracker reactivationAUD 150

Fines and tolls themselves are passed on in full to the responsible driver. Yes365 may nominate the driver as the responsible driver to Revenue NSW.

8. Accidents and damage

  • In the event of an accident, the driver must ensure safety, contact the authorities where required, not admit liability to third parties, and notify Yes365 immediately with photos and the details of any third party involved.
  • New damage identified on return (compared against the collection photos) is the driver's responsibility, up to the applicable excess.

9. Collection and return

  • On collection: full inspection with photos (exterior and interior), odometer and fuel readings, check of standard items (spare wheel, jack, warning triangle, manual, spare key) and toll tag. The driver signs the rental agreement.
  • On return: comparative inspection, settlement of the account balance (fines, tolls, damage, outstanding weeks) and bond refund as per Section 3.

10. Termination

  • Either party may terminate the rental with 2 weeks' notice.
  • Yes365 may terminate immediately in the event of persistent non-payment, prohibited use of the vehicle, loss of the driver's licence, or serious breach of these terms.
  • On termination, the vehicle must be returned to Tempe on the agreed date; failure to return the vehicle authorises Yes365 to recover it, including by means of the GPS tracker.

11. General

  • These terms are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
  • This summary does not replace the signed rental agreement, which prevails in the event of any inconsistency.
  • Yes365 may update these terms; changes do not apply retrospectively to existing agreements.
