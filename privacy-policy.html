Privacy Policy

Yes365 Rental Management Services Pty Ltd (ACN 673 976 719) ("Yes365", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Last updated: 26/08/2026

1. What personal information we collect

Depending on your relationship with us, we may collect:

  • Identity information: full name, date of birth, driver licence details (number, class, expiry) and, for international applicants, passport details;
  • Contact information: phone number, email address and residential address;
  • Business information: where provided, ABN and rideshare platform account status (Uber, DiDi);
  • Financial information: Australian bank account details for direct debit or PayTo, bond and payment/transaction history;
  • Rental records: rental agreements, vehicle condition photos taken at collection and return, odometer and fuel readings, maintenance and incident records;
  • Toll and infringement data: toll transactions and traffic infringement notices attributed to a vehicle during your rental;
  • Vehicle telematics: our vehicles are fitted with GPS tracking devices that record vehicle location. This data is collected for fleet security, recovery and operational purposes;
  • Website data: information you submit through our enquiry forms, and standard analytics/advertising data (see Section 7).

2. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you: in person at our office, over the phone or WhatsApp, through our website forms, or through our driver onboarding process. We may also receive information from toll road operators, Transport for NSW / Revenue NSW (in relation to infringements), our insurer, and rideshare platforms where you have authorised this.

3. Why we collect it

We collect, hold and use personal information to:

  • Assess your rental application, verify your identity and determine eligibility;
  • Enter into and manage your rental agreement, including billing and direct debit payments;
  • Calculate and manage your security bond and insurance excess;
  • Process tolls, infringements and insurance claims relating to your rental;
  • Maintain the safety, security and recovery of our fleet;
  • Provide customer support and communicate with you (including by SMS and WhatsApp);
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to our rental business, and we will not send you marketing communications without a means to opt out.

4. Who we disclose it to

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Our insurer and insurance broker, for policy administration and claims;
  • Our payment provider (Monova), to process payments you have authorised;
  • Toll road operators (e.g. Linkt, E-toll) and government authorities (e.g. Transport for NSW, Revenue NSW, NSW Police) in connection with tolls, infringements, accidents or as required by law;
  • Our IT and professional service providers, strictly for the operation of our systems;
  • Otherwise as required or authorised by law.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Overseas disclosure and data storage

Our systems and backups are hosted in Australia (Sydney, NSW). Some communication services we use, such as WhatsApp, email and SMS, may store messages on servers located outside Australia (for example, in the United States). Beyond this, we do not routinely disclose personal information to overseas recipients, and we take reasonable steps to ensure that any information handled by these services receives protection consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including access controls, encrypted storage and audit logging of access to driver records. We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or de-identified. Financial and contractual records are retained for 7 years.

7. Website, cookies and advertising

Our website may use cookies and analytics/advertising services (including Google Ads) to measure site performance and advertising effectiveness. These tools collect device and usage data; they do not give us access to your identity unless you submit a form.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period. If you believe we have breached the APPs, please contact us first; if you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.
